FAQ Accordion Variants

Clean accordion interactions with animated icon transitions plus a nested, section-based FAQ layout.

Clean Accordion

Can I allow more than one item to stay open?

Yes. This variant is configured for single-open behavior by default, but you can remove that rule in JavaScript if your UX needs multi-open sections.

How does the icon transition work?

The plus icon is built with pseudo-elements and smoothly rotates/fades into a minus state when each item expands.

Is this keyboard accessible?

Yes. Native <details> and <summary> provide keyboard and screen-reader support while still allowing custom styling.

Billing

Do you offer annual billing?

Yes, annual plans include a discount compared to month-to-month billing and can be changed from account settings anytime.

Can I download invoices?

Invoices are generated automatically and available under your billing history panel.

Product

Is there a dark mode?

Dark mode is available by default and can follow the system preference or be forced in settings.

Can teams share templates?

Yes. Team spaces can share reusable templates, blocks, and onboarding checklists.
